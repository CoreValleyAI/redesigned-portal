Small uppercase-mono status pill — use for instance state, region, plan, live indicators.

```jsx
<Badge tone="success" dot>running</Badge>
<Badge tone="hydro">h200</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral">np-ktm-1</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral | hydro | success | warning | danger | info`. Add `dot` for live/running statuses (it glows on hydro/success).
